Privacy Policy — Nook Pro

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Developer: SG1 Labs LLC

Overview

Nook Pro is a local-first compartment vault. Your secrets, snippets, warranty records, and documents are stored only on your Mac. Nook has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry.

Data stored on your device

  • Compartment contents: snippets, secrets, API keys, warranty records, notes, and attached documents
  • Preferences such as keyboard shortcuts, Pro unlock state, and view settings

All of this data lives inside the app's sandbox on your Mac. Nook never transmits it to SG1 Labs or to any third party.

Local inter-app queries (Hearth)

If you also use OmniChat Pro, Nook can answer warranty questions from that app through Hearth, a local inter-app layer. This is a direct file exchange between the two apps in a shared App Group container (group.com.sg1labs.hearth) on your Mac:

  • Only warranty records are shared, and only active ones by default — never secrets, credit cards, or API keys
  • Nothing leaves your Mac; no network is involved
  • You can stop the exchange at any time by quitting either app

On-device AI

Nook can use Apple's on-device intelligence (for example, semantic search over your own items). These features run entirely on your Mac. Your item contents are never sent to Apple or to SG1 Labs.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. SG1 Labs never sees your payment details, your Apple ID, or your purchase history beyond the standard App Store Connect sales reports Apple provides to developers.

Children's privacy

Nook Pro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data flows change. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
