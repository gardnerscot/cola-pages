Why “capture” is the hard part

Solopreneurs don't lose money because they forget to organize — they lose money because the thing they needed was never captured in the first place. The receipt for the client lunch. The voice memo with the good idea. The screenshot of the competitor's pricing page. By the time you're at tax season or a client call, it's gone.

The fix isn't a better filing system. It's a capture habit that's so fast it doesn't need discipline. That's what this checklist is for: figure out what a capture app actually needs to do for your brain, then compare the options.

The capture app checklist (what actually matters)

  • Capture in under 5 seconds — Siri, share sheet, clipboard, or one button. If it takes longer than a thought, you won't use it.
  • Works offline, keeps everything on your device — your receipts and ideas are nobody else's training data.
  • No account, no subscription just to store your own stuff — one-time price or free beats $10/month forever.
  • Receipts & expenses built in — merchant, amount, and date searchable for tax time.
  • Simple buckets, not a filing system — Inbox, Expenses, Projects, Tasks, Reference. ADHD-friendly means capture without organizing.
  • Search that actually finds things — full-text and Spotlight, not a folder tree you have to maintain.
  • Syncs peer-to-peer between your devices — your Mac and iPhone agree without a cloud middleman.
  • Exports when you need it — receipts to CSV for your accountant, notes to plain text.

Apple Notes is genuinely good for text — until you need a receipt, a web clip, an expense, and a voice memo to live in the same place with one search. Notion and Obsidian are powerful, but they ask you to build a system first. That's the exact opposite of what a capture app should do.

Who each one is actually for

  • Apple Notes / Google Keep — you only capture text, and you don't mind your notes living in someone's cloud.
  • Evernote — you've been using it since 2008 and the subscription is a sunk cost.
  • Notion — you enjoy building systems more than capturing things. (Be honest.)
  • Obsidian — you're a tinkerer with a vault and a plugin for everything.
  • LifeCapture — you're a solopreneur, indie hacker, or freelancer whose brain is already full, and you need receipts, ideas, and clippings captured without a system.
